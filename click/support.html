System requirements

  • macOS 14.0 Sonoma or later
  • Apple silicon or Intel
  • Screen Recording permission (System Settings › Privacy & Security)
  • Camera and Microphone permission only if you record with them
  • Microphone recording requires macOS 15 Sequoia or later

Common fixes

The capture overlay is blank or black. macOS has not granted Screen Recording. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security › Screen & System Audio Recording, enable Click, then quit and reopen it — macOS requires a relaunch after granting.

The keyboard shortcut does nothing. Another app has claimed it. macOS gives the shortcut to whichever app registered it first; free it in the other app, or change the system shortcut in System Settings › Keyboard › Keyboard Shortcuts › Screenshots.

Pro features are still locked after purchase. Open Click Settings › About and choose Restore Purchases, making sure you are signed in with the Apple Account you bought on.

The menu-bar icon is missing. macOS hides menu-bar items when the bar runs out of room. Quit an app that owns a menu-bar icon, or widen the bar, and Click reappears. The keyboard shortcuts keep working either way.
