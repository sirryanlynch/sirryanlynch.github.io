Annotate a bug report on a Mac

A short how-to from Click

A useful bug report is a picture of the problem, already marked, ready to paste into a ticket or a message. Click is a native Mac menu-bar app built for that loop: Capture, Annotate, Send.

No account. No cloud. Your captures stay on your Mac until you send them.

Capture the moment it goes wrong

Open the screen where the bug appears. Capture just that region, or the window, so the report is easy to read. The picture is ready to mark up right away.

Hide anything that should not travel with the report

A bug on a real screen often includes a name, an email, or a customer record. Blur or pixelate those bits before the image leaves your Mac, so the report is still clear and nothing private rides along.

Light up the thing that is wrong

Use spotlight to dim everything around the broken control, the bad state, or the missing result. If a short label or an arrow helps, add it. Stop when a teammate can see the problem without a long explanation.

Send the finished picture

Copy it and paste it into the ticket, the chat, or the email. The work is done in one place, and the report arrives as a finished image instead of a pile of notes.

Capture, Annotate, Send. It feels native and stays out of the way.
