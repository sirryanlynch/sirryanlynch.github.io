Redact a screenshot before you share it

A short how-to from Click

A screenshot can carry more than you meant to share: a name, an email, a token, a message that was not part of the point. Redact those details before the picture goes anywhere.

Click is a native Mac menu-bar app for this. Capture, Annotate, Send. No account. No cloud. Your captures stay on your Mac until you send them.

Capture first, then look again

Capture the region or window you actually want to send. Then look at it the way a stranger would. Anything you would not paste in plain text should not sit in the picture either.

Cover the sensitive bits

Blur or pixelate names, addresses, keys, and other private text. Check the edges of the selection so a fragment does not remain readable. The rest of the image can stay sharp, so the point of the screenshot is still easy to see.

Send only the finished image

Once the private details are covered, copy the picture and paste it where it needs to go. The original stays on your Mac; what you send is the version you reviewed.

Capture, Annotate, Send. It feels native and stays out of the way.
