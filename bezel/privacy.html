Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 September 2026

Bezel is a screenshot and screen-recording app for macOS. It is built so that your captures never leave your Mac unless you send them somewhere yourself.

What Bezel never collects

  • No screenshots or recordings, and nothing about their contents — no pixels, no text read out of an image, no window titles, no file names.
  • No account. Bezel has no sign-up, no login and no user profile.
  • No advertising identifiers, and no tracking of you across other apps or websites.
  • No contacts, location, browsing history, or file listings outside the folder you choose for captures.

Anonymous usage statistics

Bezel sends anonymous product-interaction signals so we can see which features people actually use. A signal is an event name from a fixed list we wrote — the app launched, an annotation tool was used, a capture mode was chosen — plus short labels we choose ourselves, such as arrow or region. Nothing you type, capture or open is ever included.

These signals are not linked to your identity and are not used to track you. Our analytics processor is TelemetryDeck, which is designed so that individual users cannot be identified or re-identified.

You can turn this off. Open Bezel Settings › General and switch off Share anonymous usage statistics. It is on by default; switching it off stops the analytics SDK from starting at all, so nothing is sent.

What stays on your Mac

Captures are written to the folder you pick in Settings. The recents list, your tool defaults and your preferences are stored in Bezel's own sandbox container on your Mac and are deleted when you delete the app.

Network requests

Bezel makes network requests in exactly three cases:

  • Anonymous usage signals, as described above, while that setting is on.
  • The newsletter, if you opt in by entering your address in Settings › About. Your address is sent to our mailing provider, MailerLite, and used only to email you about Bezel. There is an unsubscribe link in every message.
  • Opening a link you clicked, such as this policy, in your browser.

Bezel has no updater of its own — the Mac App Store handles updates. Nothing else is uploaded, and there is no background sync.

Purchases

Bezel Pro is sold only through the Mac App Store. Purchases are processed by Apple, who is the seller. Bezel receives an entitlement from Apple that says whether Pro is active; it never receives your name, address or payment details.

System permissions

macOS requires Screen Recording permission for any screen capture, including still screenshots. Camera and Microphone permissions are requested only when you enable them for a recording. All three are granted by you in System Settings and can be revoked there at any time.

Your rights

Because Bezel holds no account, there is normally nothing of yours for us to export or delete. If you have emailed support or joined the newsletter, write to SirRyanLynch@gmail.com and we will delete that correspondence and your address on request. Anonymous usage signals carry no identifier, so they cannot be traced back to you or individually deleted — switching the setting off stops any further signals.

Children

Bezel is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date above changes and the new version is published here before it takes effect.
